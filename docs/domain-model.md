# Domain Model

This is the minimum proposed domain model for TCMD V1. Field lists are deliberately small and are not a database design.

## Relationship overview

```text
Course 1 -------- many TrainingGroups
Instructor 1 ---- many TrainingGroups
Student 1 ------- many Enrollments
TrainingGroup 1 - many Enrollments
TrainingGroup 1 - many TrainingSessions
Enrollment 1 ---- many AttendanceRecords
TrainingSession 1 many AttendanceRecords
StaffUser 0..1 -- 0..1 Instructor
```

Student and TrainingGroup have a many-to-many relationship through Enrollment. AttendanceRecord connects an Enrollment to a TrainingSession.

## StaffUser

Represents a person who can sign in to TCMD.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- display name;
- unique sign-in name or email;
- password credential information managed securely;
- role;
- active status; and
- optional instructor identifier.

Rules:

- Sign-in identity must be unique.
- Plain-text passwords must never be stored.
- An inactive user cannot sign in.
- A staff user may link to at most one instructor record.
- A staff user has exactly one role in V1. Multiple roles are deferred.

## Student

Represents a person who receives training.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- unique, system-generated student number in the format `STU-000001`;
- full name;
- required phone number;
- optional email address;
- active status; and
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- A student can have many enrollments.
- An inactive student cannot receive a new enrollment.
- Historical enrollments and attendance remain connected to an inactive student.
- Student number is generated by TCMD and cannot be changed by normal users.

## Instructor

Represents a person who teaches training groups.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- full name;
- contact information;
- active status; and
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- An instructor can be the primary instructor of many groups.
- An inactive instructor cannot be assigned to a new group.
- An instructor may optionally have one linked staff account.
- A Planned group may temporarily have no instructor. One active primary instructor is required before activation.

## Course

Represents a reusable training subject or offering. It is not a particular scheduled delivery.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- unique course code;
- name;
- optional description;
- active status; and
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- Course code must be unique.
- Course code is trimmed, stored in invariant uppercase, and is unique without regard to the casing supplied by a user.
- Course code is limited to 50 characters and may contain letters, digits, spaces, hyphens, underscores, periods, and slashes.
- Course name is limited to 200 characters, and the optional description is limited to 2,000 characters.
- Course-code uniqueness applies across active and inactive courses.
- A course can have many training groups.
- An inactive course cannot be used for a new group.
- Course pricing, prerequisites, and materials are outside V1.

## TrainingGroup

Represents one cohort receiving a particular course over a period of time.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- group name;
- course identifier;
- optional primary instructor identifier while Planned;
- planned start date;
- planned end date;
- status; and
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- A group belongs to exactly one course.
- A group has at most one primary instructor, which is required before activation.
- A group can have many enrollments and sessions.
- Planned end date cannot be before planned start date.
- Group statuses are Planned, Active, Completed, and Cancelled.
- The same course cannot have two groups with the same name and planned start date.
- V1 supports at most one primary instructor per group. Multiple instructors are deferred.
- New groups begin as Planned. Planned groups may change course and may assign, replace, or remove their primary instructor.
- Active groups cannot change course or remove their instructor, but may replace the instructor with another active instructor.
- Planned and Active group names and dates may be updated. Completed and Cancelled groups cannot be edited.
- A group moves from Planned to Active and then to Completed, or from Planned or Active to Cancelled. Completed and Cancelled are terminal.
- Activation requires the assigned course and primary instructor to be active. Existing inactive references remain for history when they are not changed.
- Group names are trimmed and limited to 200 characters. Duplicate-name comparison follows the database's case-insensitive behavior.
- Cancellation preserves a group and its history; TrainingGroup does not have a separate active flag.

## Enrollment

Represents one student's membership in one training group.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- student identifier;
- training-group identifier;
- enrollment date;
- status; and
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- An enrollment belongs to exactly one student and one group.
- The same student cannot have two enrollment records for the same group.
- New enrollments require an active student and an eligible group.
- Changing status does not delete attendance history.
- Statuses are Active, Completed, and Withdrawn.
- A withdrawn student who rejoins the same group reuses the existing enrollment, which returns to Active.

## TrainingSession

Represents one scheduled meeting of a training group.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- training-group identifier;
- session date;
- start time;
- end time;
- status; and
- optional plain-text location;
- created and last-updated timestamps.

Rules:

- A session belongs to exactly one group.
- End time must be later than start time.
- A group can have many sessions.
- A cancelled session remains in history.
- A session date must fall within the group's planned dates.
- Statuses are Scheduled, Completed, and Cancelled.
- Structured rooms and schedule-conflict checks are deferred.

## AttendanceRecord

Represents one enrolled student's attendance at one session.

Minimum proposed information:

- identifier;
- enrollment identifier;
- training-session identifier;
- attendance status;
- optional note;
- recorded timestamp; and
- last-updated timestamp.

Rules:

- The enrollment and session must belong to the same training group.
- There can be at most one record for an enrollment in a session.
- Attendance cannot normally be recorded for a cancelled session.
- Attendance cannot be recorded before the session starts.
- Statuses are Present, Absent, Late, and Excused.
- A correction note is optional.
- Store the staff user who created and last updated the record. Full correction-history auditing is deferred.

## Cross-entity business rules

- Referenced records must exist.
- Historical records should not be physically deleted when other records depend on them.
- Inactive records remain readable for history but are not available for new assignments.
- Group dates and session date/time use the training center's local time. System metadata timestamps use UTC. Multi-time-zone conversion is deferred.
- Created/updated timestamps should exist on important records.
- Attendance records require created-by and updated-by staff identifiers. Other V1 records require timestamps but not actor auditing.
- Important mutable records use SQL Server `rowversion` so concurrent changes are rejected instead of silently overwritten.
- Database constraints and server validation should both protect important uniqueness and relationship rules.
